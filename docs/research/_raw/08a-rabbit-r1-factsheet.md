## 10. Root causes (analysis backed by the evidence above)
1. **Overpromise** — pitched a LAM that "operates apps"; integrations broke at launch; later shown to be an Android app + cloud browser sessions. Credibility lost before shipping.
2. **No reason to carry a second device** — ~5% of buyers used it daily; the CEO: "no one wants to carry around two devices".
3. **Weak security culture** — fleet-wide hard-coded keys, non-deletable on-device logs, conflicting disclosure stories.
4. **Business model** — one-time $199 couldn't fund open-ended cloud LLM/TTS/STT costs (Perplexity, ElevenLabs, OpenAI, Anthropic); later an expensive subscription (Intern $99.99/mo) and payroll problems from 2025 (causal link = inference).
5. **Founder credibility** — the GAMA/NFT history magnified every later problem.
6. **Regional expansion without regulatory readiness** — India units ready May 2024, unnamed regulatory problems wiped out expected revenue. Directly relevant to PersonaAgent's US + India v1: plan BIS/WPC certification early; don't make cash flow depend on a hardware launch.

Also: Rabbit itself added meeting recording with offline transcripts/summaries (rabbitOS 2) — validates PersonaAgent's memory pillar but signals commoditization of that feature.
